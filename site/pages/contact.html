What to write about

  • Something doesn't work. Please mention the device (phone, tablet or computer), the browser, and what your child was doing when it happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
  • Ideas. A new outfit, a place on the map, a book for the library — children's ideas are especially welcome, and many features started that way.
  • Accessibility. If a button is too small, a colour is hard to see or something is confusing, we want to know.
  • Printables and classrooms. Questions about using the paper dolls with a group.
  • Privacy. Anything about the Privacy Policy.

Before you write

Many common questions — devices, saving, internet, ages — are answered in the FAQ.

Please don't send children's personal information. If your child wants to send an idea, a first name and age is plenty.
