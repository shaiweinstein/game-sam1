How to make them

  1. Print on card paper (cardstock) if you have it — the dolls stand up better and last longer. Normal printer paper works too; glue it onto a cereal box for extra strength.
  2. Cut out Lily together with the grey stand tab under her feet. Fold the tab back and she can stand up on a table or shelf.
  3. Cut out the clothes, keeping the grey tabs attached. Small hands do best with the big pieces first; a grown-up can help with the shoes and the Cat Ears.
  4. Fold the grey tabs on the clothes along the dashed lines and tape them on Lily's back.
  5. Shoes, hats and bags have no tabs: a small piece of tape or re-usable putty underneath is enough.
  6. Dress her for wherever she's going today — and write your child's name on the line that says "This Lily belongs to…".

Ideas

  • Design your own clothes: lay a printed top on plain paper, trace around it, and draw a new pattern inside.
  • Keep the pieces in an envelope taped to the back of a notebook — a travel game for waiting rooms and car trips.
  • Build a little stage from a shoebox and put on a fashion show with the dolls.
  • Match the paper outfit to the same one in the game's wardrobe.

Printing from inside the game
