What to play

A home with a wardrobe and a kitchen, a little town with a library, and a whole beach out front. Each activity has its own page with tips for playing together.

  • 👗 Dress-up

    Six hairstyles, eight tops, eight bottoms, six pairs of shoes, six swimsuits and a drawer of extras, for Lily and her three friends. Tap to try a look on, and hit Undo whenever it isn't right.

  • 👩‍🍳 Kitchen

    Apples and bananas, warm soup, fluffy pancakes, juice and smoothies. Every treat fills Lily's energy bar so there's always room for another adventure.

  • 📚 Storybook library

    28 picture books you can read — and hear. Every page has a "Read page" button with a warm narrator, from short Level 1 stories to Beatrix Potter classics. See all 28 books.

  • 🏄 Beach play

    Press and hold the water to swim, catch a wave on the surfboard, putter around in the duck boat, or play catch with the beach ball.

  • 🏰 Sandcastle builder

    Pile, carve and smooth real sand, stamp towers, walls, gates and stairs, and plant a flag. Press Done and the castle stays on the beach.

  • ✂️ Printable paper dolls

    Take Lily off the screen: print a free paper doll in any of her six hairstyles plus her whole wardrobe with fold tabs — 35 pieces to cut out and dress up by hand.

How to play

There's no tutorial to lose anyone in — the buttons say what they do.

  • 🖱️ Press and hold on the sea to swim toward your finger, and drag while holding to steer. Release to float and look around.
  • 🏰 Drag to build in the sandcastle builder: pick a tool below the plot, then drag on the sand to pile, carve or smooth. One tap stamps a tower.
  • 🍎 Big buttons everywhere — dress-up tabs, foods, activities and map places are all one tap; if Lily runs low on energy, feed her and go back.

Works in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox — on phones, tablets and computers, with touch or mouse. No app store, no plugin, no install. See what's new.
