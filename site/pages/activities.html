Activities

Lily's day has no fixed order and no goals to reach. A child can spend the whole visit trying on outfits, or go straight to the beach and never come back. These pages describe each activity in more detail — what the child actually does, what they are practising along the way, and small ideas for playing together.

  • 👗 Dress-up

    Hairstyles, tops, bottoms, shoes, swimsuits and extras for Lily and her three friends.

  • 👩‍🍳 Kitchen

    Eight foods and drinks that refill Lily's energy for the next trip around town.

  • 📚 Storybook library

    28 picture books with a narrator who reads every page aloud.

  • 🏄 Beach play

    Swimming, surfing, the duck boat and a game of catch on a 3D beach.

  • 🏰 Sandcastle builder

    Sculpt sand, stamp towers and walls, and leave a castle on the beach.

  • ✂️ Printable paper dolls

    Screen-free: print Lily and her whole wardrobe, cut out and dress up by hand.

Getting around: the town map

Between activities, Lily walks across a small town map: Home, the Park, School, the Library, the Toy Shop, Grandma's house and the Beach. Tap a place and she walks there along the roads and says something about it when she arrives ("Grandma gives the best hugs! 💜"). Walking uses a little of her energy — longer trips use more — so every now and then she needs a snack from the kitchen. That small loop of going out, getting hungry and coming home to eat is the only "rule" in the game, and it gives younger children a gentle sense of cause and effect without any way to fail.
