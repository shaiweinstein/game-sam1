📚 The storybook library

These are the 28 picture books on the shelf in Lily's library. Every one can be read in the game, and every page can be read aloud by the narrator. How the reader works is described on the library activity page.

The first 18 books come from StoryWeaver, a free, openly licensed children's library by Pratham Books, and are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. The last 10 are Beatrix Potter classics, which are in the public domain. Each entry links to the book's original page. Full attributions are on the credits page.
