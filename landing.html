What to play

Four rooms at home and a whole beach out front. Here's what a visit usually looks like.

  • 👗 Dress-up

    Six hairstyles, eight tops, eight bottoms, six pairs of shoes, six swimsuits and a drawer of extras. Tap to try a look on, and hit Undo whenever it isn't right. Then pick which of the four friends you're playing as today.

  • 👩‍🍳 Kitchen

    Cook and serve: apples and bananas, warm soup, fluffy pancakes, juice and smoothies. Every treat fills Lily's energy bar so there's always room for another adventure. Veggies first and pancakes last — that part is between you two.

  • 📚 28 storybooks you can read — and hear

    There's a real little library inside the game with 28 picture books — gentle originals and Beatrix Potter classics like The Tale of Peter Rabbit. Every page has a "Read page" button, so a warm narrator reads it aloud — a cozy read-along for bedtime, and a big help as kids start reading themselves. The short Level 1–3 books suit early readers, and like everything here the library is free: no install, no sign-up.

  • 🏄 Beach play

    The 3D beach is where the hour goes: press and hold the water to swim (head-up float or freestyle stroke), catch a wave on the surfboard, putter around in the duck boat, or play catch with the beach ball.

  • 🏰 Sandcastle builder

    Sculpt real sand: pile it up, carve it back, smooth the slopes, then stamp towers, walls, gates and stairs and plant a flag on top. Not sure where to start? The Keep, Fort and Mound templates lay down a ground plan you can build on — then press Done and the castle stays on the beach.

  • 🐚 Collect & care

    Notice the shells and starfish dotted in the sand, keep an eye on the energy bar and pop back to the kitchen when it runs low, and come back to a beach that remembers you: the castle you built is exactly where you left it.

For parents

This game was built for one kid who wanted a dress-up friend and a beach — the safety choices below are the honest ones, written by the person who wrote the game.

🙋

No account, no sign-up

No email address, no username, no password. The game starts the moment the page opens, for anyone.

🚫

No chat, no strangers

There is nothing user-generated: no chat, no usernames shown to others, no community, no links out. Lily can't talk to anyone but her friends inside the game.

🔒

No trackers or ads in the game itself

The game page runs entirely on your device: zero third-party scripts, no analytics, no ad slots. The only ads on this site are on this landing page, clearly labelled.

💾

Saves stay on your device

Outfits, energy and sandcastles are stored privately in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is ever uploaded — clear the browser's site data and the save is gone, no questions asked.

🔒

Nothing is ever uploaded

No accounts, no scores, nothing sent anywhere — outfits, snacks and your sandcastles live only in this browser on your device. The game downloads everything it needs from this one site and nowhere else; once it has loaded, play continues with no further connection needed.

👆

Mouse or touch, big buttons

Every control is a large, rounded, labelled button sized for small fingers — one hand, no keyboard skills needed, on phone, tablet or computer.

🧒

Friendly for ~ages 4–9

No scores, no timers, no fail states, nothing scary — just dressing up, cooking, and beach play. Best with a grown-up nearby, as with any screen.

💛

Completely free

No purchases, no subscriptions, no "premium outfits". Ads on this landing page (never in the game) keep it that way.

How to play

There's no tutorial to lose anyone in — the buttons say what they do.

  • 🖱️ Press and hold on the sea to swim toward your finger, and drag while holding to steer. Release to float and look around.
  • 🏰 Drag to build in the sandcastle builder: pick a tool below the plot, then drag on the sand to pile, carve or smooth. One tap stamps a tower.
  • 🍎 Big buttons everywhere — dress-up tabs, foods, activities and map places are all one tap; if Lily runs low on energy, feed her and go back.

Works in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox — on phones, tablets and computers, with touch or mouse. No app store, no plugin, no install.

Frequently asked questions

The questions actual parents have asked — answered honestly.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy, nothing to unlock with money, and no account. The game is a passion project; the clearly-labelled ads on this landing page exist so it can stay free for everyone.

Do we need internet to play?

You need a connection to open the site and, the first time you reach the 3D beach, to load its models (one-time, from this server only). After that, playing is fully on-device: nothing leaves the browser and nothing else comes in. A warm browser cache may let the page reopen offline, so for car trips we suggest loading the beach once before you go.

What data does it collect about my kid?

Nothing. The game stores outfits, energy and your sandcastle locally in the browser (localStorage) on your own device; there are no accounts, no analytics and no trackers in the game. When advertising is enabled on this landing page, Google's ad cookies work the way the Privacy Policy describes — always non-personalized, since this site is child-directed.

Which devices work?

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or laptop made in the last decade or so — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. The 3D beach uses standard WebGL like most browser games; the rest of the game runs on anything.

Whose artwork is this?

All the art — Lily, the outfits, the beach and the sandcastle — was drawn and modelled originally for this project (Blender + a small SVG pipeline), and the game is built on the open-source three.js and Phaser engines, properly credited in our third-party notices.

Why are there ads on the page?

One honest reason: ad income on this landing page pays for hosting and lets the game grow — new outfits, new games — while staying free. Ads never appear inside the game itself, never sit next to the play controls, and because the site is child-directed they are contextual (non-personalized) only.

What ages is it for?

About 4 to 9. Little ones love the big buttons — and the read-aloud stories with a grown-up — while older kids read the short Level 1–3 books themselves and build bigger sandcastles.

Do we need to install or sign up?

No. There is nothing to download and no app store — open the page and play. No account, no email address, no password, on a phone, tablet or computer.

What books are in the library?

28 free picture books: gentle originals and Beatrix Potter classics like The Tale of Peter Rabbit, in short Level 1–3 lengths — and every page can be read aloud by a warm narrator.
